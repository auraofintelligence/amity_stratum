A speculative browser dashboard exploring a subterranean civilisation and advanced-manufacturing pathway imagined from Amity Point on Minjerribah. The site calls itself **The Amity Archives: Subterranean Blueprint** and deliberately frames the work as a thought experiment.

## Public page

[Explore The Amity Archives](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/amity_stratum/).

## What is here

The single page moves between four lenses:

- **Vision** — the civilisational scale and long horizon;
- **Fabrication** — advanced local manufacturing concepts;
- **Resources** — proposed material and energy flows; and
- **Infrastructure** — tunnelling and subterranean construction scenarios.

Interactive charts and diagrams compare candidate systems and help visitors move through the ideas without reading a linear report.

Open `index.html` in a browser for a local view. Tailwind CSS, Chart.js, Plotly and fonts are loaded from external services.

## Status and boundaries

The subterranean city does not exist. This repository is speculative design, not an engineering design, planning application, mining proposal, land agreement, business case or construction commitment.

No site control, cultural authority, community assent, environmental approval, technical feasibility, finance or partnership should be inferred. Each of those would need its own evidence, review and decision process before a real-world pathway could be described.
